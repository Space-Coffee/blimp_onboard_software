# Flight modes

## Manual

Thottle applies to all the motors equally.

Positive yaw causes left motors to increase speed, and right ones to decrease. It also rotates front left/right servos to rotate clockwise, and back ones - counterclockwise. We should check it it will be possible - that is, will it collide with construction, or is it out of the servos' ranges.

Positive elevation rotates all servos downwards.
